MITOCHONDRIA & LONGEVITY

What can mitochondrial DNA
tell us about life?

Mito-Minerva probes mitochondrial tRNA structure with a DNA language model. Structure recovery improves after fine-tuning. A lifespan analysis across 1,321 species finds no association after controlling for phylogeny: an informative negative result.
